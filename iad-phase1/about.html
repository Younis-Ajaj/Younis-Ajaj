About DataPath

A student-built project that turns scattered learning material into one clear path.

Our mission

DataPath Jordan helps Data Science & AI students at Jordanian universities learn in the right order, practise on real projects and connect with peers — all for free.

The idea came from our own first year: we spent more time searching for what to learn than actually learning it.

Our values

  • Clarity — one plan, clearly ordered
  • Quality — every resource is reviewed
  • Practice — learn by building projects
  • Community — nobody learns alone
