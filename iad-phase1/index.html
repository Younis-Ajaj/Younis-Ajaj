Why DataPath?

Most students in Jordan learn data science from scattered YouTube playlists and outdated PDFs. Without a plan they jump between topics, skip the maths and reach graduation without a portfolio.

  • A single ordered plan instead of hundreds of random links
  • Resources checked by senior students and filtered by level
  • Local events that connect students with each other and industry
  • Portfolio-first: every stage ends with a project for GitHub
